05The workflow

How a film is built

The looks share one pipeline. The look is settled in the first step, the treatment, and everything after it is generated under that decision: the storyboard, every shot, the narration, the cut and the 4K 60 fps render.

01 kleo_adapt_prompt

Treatment

Your assistant hands the request to kleo_adapt_prompt, which reads it against the intake: subject, length, format, look, film or animatic, the AI upscale, music, subtitles and the narration language, plus audience, tone, what must appear and your own photos as options, and asks for whatever is missing in one message instead of assuming it. Back comes the treatment: logline, angle, opening, acts, ending, look, pacing, narrator. The look is decided here, before a frame exists.

02 kleo_storyboard_guide

Storyboard

kleo_storyboard_guide sets out how the storyboard is written under that treatment: a direction block, the scenes, the shots and the limits.

03 kleo_create_video

Footage and narration

kleo_create_video checks everything, states the exact credits and starts the render on a GPU rented for that job; nothing is charged until it accepts. Nano Banana Pro draws every frame in the look the treatment chose, and a vision model checks each one against your request; in a film Seedance 2.5 then turns every frame into moving footage, in an animatic the camera moves over each drawn frame. One narrator voices it, in English or Italian, with the music track and burned-in subtitles you asked for.

04 kleo_get_result

Render

The shots and narration are cut together into one film. Agree on any available graphic treatment before rendering. An MP4 at 4K 60 fps, 3840×2160 for 16:9 or 2160×3840 for 9:16, typically 25–35 minutes on the rented GPU. Your assistant follows the progress and kleo_get_result hands it the link, good for 7 days.

The showcases have different creative treatments. Confirm the sound, subtitle and graphic options available for your own order; a sample’s treatment does not establish what every standard film includes. The current Terms specify a small Kleo mark on the video. Review the output conditions before purchasing.

06How a scene is cut

Not a slideshow.
A line, cut into shots.

Each narrated line becomes a scene, and each scene is cut into shots. Kleo writes a description for every shot, generates it from its own frame, and times the cut to a word you actually hear. Watch the line below.

narration · 9 s
shot 01The captain burying a chest on a golden beach at sunset.Captain
shot 02She walks away along the shoreline, the chest gone.never
shot 03The same beach at night, empty.nobody

The same character is described the same way in every shot, so the captain still looks like the captain three shots later.
