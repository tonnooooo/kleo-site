Know the cost.
Then create.

One-off credit packs in EUR, no subscription. One credit covers two seconds of film, with a minimum of 10 credits per render. Both looks, realistic and animation, cost the same: the length is the only price.

Three packs.

€40 100 credits

Enough for a 3-minute film at 90 credits, with 10 credits left.

Your starter balance.

Connecting Kleo gives you 7 credits, with no form and no card. A film costs at least 10 credits, so the starter credits alone do not buy one: add the €5 pack and your 17-credit balance covers a 30-second Short, which costs 15 credits.

Calculate the credits.

60 seconds = 30 credits

15 to 300 seconds. One credit for every two seconds, rounded up, with a 10-credit minimum. The same rule applies to the realistic and the animation look, in 16:9 or 9:16. Packs are bought separately on your account page.

Buying and using credits.

  1. Connect Kleo and ask the assistant for your account page: it shows your balance and the checkout links.
  2. Buy a pack through the Stripe checkout on that page. Packs are one-off purchases in EUR; there is no subscription.
  3. Ask for the film’s cost before you approve a render: kleo_create_video states the exact credits first, and nothing is charged until it accepts the job.

A 5-minute film costs 150 credits: a single 100-credit pack does not cover it. Your available balance and the account checkout are the final reference before purchase.

A refused call charges nothing. If you cancel a render while it is running, the unspent credits are refunded.

What the price includes.

One render is one complete narrated film, rendered on a GPU rented for that job. It typically takes 25 to 35 minutes; the assistant reports the progress and hands you the link.

  • FilmA narrated film with every shot generated as moving footage, in the realistic or the animation look.
  • OutputAn MP4 in 4K (3840×2160 or 2160×3840) at 60 fps, 16:9 for YouTube or 9:16 for a Short.
  • NarrationOne narrator, in English or Italian. No music, no captions, no on-screen text.
  • Length15 seconds to 5 minutes.
  • DeliveryA download link for the finished MP4, good for 7 days: keep a local copy. Kleo never publishes to a channel.
