01How it works

Your vision. Every frame.

01 your words

Direct the story

Tell your assistant the subject, the length and the format. Kleo reads the brief and asks the rest in one message: the look, film or animatic, the AI upscale, music, subtitles and the narration language.

02 kleo_adapt_prompt

Read the treatment

Your assistant shows you the treatment first: logline, angle, opening, acts and ending. Change what you want before a frame is made.

03 kleo_create_video

Kleo renders

Under that treatment the storyboard is written and Nano Banana Pro draws every frame, each one checked against your request. In a film, Seedance 2.5 turns every frame into moving footage; in an animatic the camera moves over each drawn frame instead. One narrator voices it, in English or Italian. The exact credit cost is stated before anything is charged; nothing is charged until the render is accepted.

04Behind the scenes

You direct. Kleo renders.

Stay in your assistant for the brief, the progress and the link: Kleo works behind the conversation. Nano Banana Pro draws the frames, a vision model checks each one against your request, and in a film Seedance 2.5 turns every frame into moving footage — no slideshow of stills — while one narrator carries it. The render happens at 4K 60 fps on a GPU rented for your job, typically 25–35 minutes. The finished MP4 is the deliverable; you choose when and where to publish it.

  • TreatmentYour brief becomes a logline, an angle, an opening, acts and an ending — shown to you before a frame is made.
  • StoryboardDirection, scenes and shots, written under that treatment; the exact credits are stated before anything is charged.
  • FramesGoogle Nano Banana Pro draws every frame; your own photos can become the character references. A vision model checks each picture against your request before it is used.
  • FootageIn a film, ByteDance Seedance 2.5 generates every shot as moving footage from its own frame, in the realistic or animation look. In an animatic, the same drawn frames with the camera moving over each — no generated clip.
  • SoundOne narrator, in English or Italian, carries the film. A music track and burned-in subtitles when you ask for them. The current Terms specify a small Kleo mark on exported videos.
  • FinishAn MP4 at 4K 60 fps, 16:9 or 9:16, on a link your assistant hands you — good for 7 days. For a film, an optional AI upscale (Real-ESRGAN + RIFE) sharpens every shot.

“Use Kleo to make a 60-second narrated film about how coffee travels from farm to cup. Realistic look, English narration, 16:9. Show me the treatment and the credit cost before starting the render.”

07FAQ

Before your first film.

What does Kleo actually create?

Two products from one storyboard. The film: a complete narrated film, in a realistic look (generated live-action footage) or an animation look (a 2D animated film), every shot generated as moving footage by Seedance 2.5 from a Nano Banana Pro frame. The animatic: the same treatment, storyboard and drawn frames, the camera moving over each frame, the same narrator and graphic layer, no generated clip — 15 to 60 seconds, also the film’s preview. With your assistant Kleo writes the treatment and the storyboard, narrates and renders an MP4 at 4K 60 fps. You get a download link in the assistant, good for 7 days.

How long can a film be?

From 15 seconds to 5 minutes; an animatic from 15 to 60 seconds. Choose 16:9 for YouTube or 9:16 for a vertical Short; both render at 4K (3840×2160 or 2160×3840) and 60 fps.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. New accounts receive 5 starter credits, with no card required: one animatic of up to 20 seconds. A film requires a credit-pack purchase: the €5 pack adds 10 purchased credits, exactly a 15-second film. During the launch, a code (PRODUCTHUNT, HN, REDDIT, X or TIKTOK) opens one free 15-second film before any pack, for the first 30 accounts per code. Confirm your usable film balance and quote before rendering.

Does Kleo include narration, music and captions?

Yes, when you want them. Kleo asks in its intake: narration in English (the default) or Italian, a music track, burned-in subtitles. Your answers show in the treatment you approve. The current Terms specify a small Kleo mark on the video.

Will Kleo publish to my channel automatically?

No. Kleo hands your assistant a link to the finished MP4; you review it, download it and upload it yourself. Kleo never posts to YouTube, TikTok or any other channel.

Which looks can I choose?

Two. Realistic is generated live-action footage; animation is a 2D animated film. Name the look in your brief and your assistant passes it on — the price depends on the length only, not the look. See the two looks.
