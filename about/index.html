the assistant you already use.

Kleo is a remote MCP server that turns a brief into a complete narrated film, realistic or animated, from Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Cursor or any other MCP client.

What Kleo is

Kleo lives at one address, https://mcp.kleooai.com/mcp. You connect it once, then ask for a film in the conversation. Your assistant writes the treatment and the storyboard with Kleo’s tools; Kleo checks the request, states the exact credits before anything is charged, renders on a rented GPU and hands back a link to the finished MP4, good for 7 days.

Kleo makes one kind of video, a narrated film: 4K at 60 fps, 16:9 for YouTube or 9:16 for a Short, from 15 seconds to 5 minutes, with one narrator in English or Italian. Two looks are live on the server: realistic, generated live-action footage, and animation, a 2D animated film. Every shot is generated as moving footage.

What is real today

  • Two sample filmsTether, 20 seconds in one shot, and the opening minute of Signal Delay, a documentary rendered with Kleo’s realistic look. Both are 4K 60 fps masters shown at 1080p.
  • Two looksRealistic and animation, both live on the server. The samples are realistic; an animation sample will be published.
  • The price1 credit is 2 seconds of film, rounded up, 10 credits minimum. Packs, one-off and in EUR: €5 = 10 credits, €15 = 35, €40 = 100. Connecting gives 7 credits, with no form and no card. Seven is below the 10-credit minimum; with the €5 pack it becomes 17, a 30-second Short.
  • Not thereNo app, no dashboard: Kleo is only the server behind your assistant. No background music, no captions and no on-screen text in the standard film. Kleo never publishes to YouTube, TikTok or any channel: you download the MP4 and post it yourself.
