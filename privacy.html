What Kleo knows
about you.

Last updated 25 September 2026

Kleo is operated by Kevyn Wahuzue trading as Kanaky Tech, New Zealand, NZBN 9429053554017. This notice covers kleooai.com, the Kleo server and its assistant integrations.

Connecting does not require an email or password. Kleo receives the requests your assistant sends to it, the images you choose to supply, and the information needed to render videos and manage credits. Buying credits or contacting support can also provide your email.

The public website has no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. The account service uses an essential sign-in cookie.

What we collect and why

  • Account and connection: an account identifier, credit balance, timestamps, a signed account cookie and OAuth permissions, to recognise your account and authorise your assistant.
  • Your creative request: prompts, requirements, treatments, storyboards, narration choices, reference pictures and generated descriptions, to plan and produce the content you request.
  • Render records: progress, outputs, errors, credit charges and refunds, to deliver files, control usage and resolve problems.
  • Payment records: Stripe transaction identifiers, amount, currency, status, purchased credits and the checkout email and country when provided. Stripe processes card details; Kleo does not receive the full card number.
  • Support: the messages and contact details you choose to send, to answer requests and handle complaints.
  • Security: the application uses a keyed fingerprint of the connecting IP address for account-creation limits. Hosting and network providers necessarily process connection information, and may keep their own security logs.

Kleo receives only information sent to its tools or service, not unrestricted access to your entire ChatGPT or other assistant history. A tool request may include text or images from the conversation. Do not submit sensitive information that is unnecessary for your video, and only supply pictures or information about others when you have the right to do so.

Your choices

You choose whether to connect an assistant, supply references, buy credits or contact support. Without the relevant request and account information, Kleo cannot render a video or allocate its credits. References are optional. Review the treatment, product and quoted credits before authorising a render.

You can disconnect Kleo through your assistant's settings. Disconnecting stops that connection; it does not by itself delete your account, payment records or stored content.

Your account and cookies

The server uses an essential cookie called kleo_id, lasting up to about 400 days, to remember a signed account identifier. An anonymous account can contain an internal placeholder email ending in @anon.kleo.invalid; it is not a contact address.

Your private Kleo key can restore access in another browser. Anyone holding it can use your account and spend credits. Keep it private; do not paste it into public conversations or support messages. Clearing the cookie without keeping access to your key can make the account inaccessible.

Who processes information

  • Cloudflare: server hosting, account data, storage, security and AI planning or image processing.
  • Vast.ai and its compute providers: rendering on rented machines, which receive the material needed for the job.
  • kie.ai: image and moving-footage generation from prompts and reference frames. Both films and animatics may use its image generation; an animatic does not order moving clips.
  • GitHub Pages: public website hosting and associated connection requests.
  • Stripe: checkout, payment processing, fraud prevention and payment records.
  • Your chosen assistant provider, including OpenAI for ChatGPT: your conversation, tool requests and results under that provider's own terms and privacy notice.
  • Google Fonts: some pages load fonts from Google's servers, which receive normal browser connection information. The homepage serves its fonts locally.

These providers can process data outside New Zealand. Processing locations depend on hosting and the compute available for a job. Their own privacy terms also apply to their services. Contact us if you need information about a particular provider or overseas processing before submitting content.

Kanaky Tech does not sell your personal information or use your prompts and videos to train its own models. External AI and assistant providers process information under their own policies; this is not a promise about every provider's retention or model-training practices.

Access, correction, deletion and complaints

The privacy contact is Kevyn Wahuzue, trading as Kanaky Tech. Email k.wahuzuepro@gmail.com for access to your personal information, correction, a deletion request or a privacy complaint. Product support is also available at kleooai@gmail.com.

Use your account number, video number or transaction reference, never your private Kleo key. We may need to verify that you control the account before disclosing or deleting information. Deletion can remove access to content and credits; applicable consumer and record-keeping rights remain in force.

You have rights under New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020. If we cannot resolve your concern, you can contact the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner. Other mandatory rights may apply where you live.
