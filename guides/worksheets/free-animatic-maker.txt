REFERENCE NOTES
GIVE THE PREVIEW ONE JOB

Use the first animatic to answer a small creative question. For example: can a viewer understand that a paper boat has returned to the person who made it? That is a story test you can judge from scene order, narration and recognisable visual details. It does not require a promise that a generated character or object will stay identical across future motion clips.

The outline below is a fictional planning example, not an executed prompt or a report of a Kleo render. Ask your assistant to turn it into a treatment, then check that the chosen product is an animatic before approving production.

A 30-second paper-boat story to plan before rendering

Approximate beat: 0–8 seconds: departure
Picture intention: A maker folds a paper boat beside a window. Give the boat one simple identifying detail.
What to check: Can the viewer identify the maker and the boat without a long explanation?

Approximate beat: 8–21 seconds: journey
Picture intention: Two clear locations suggest the boat’s journey. Narration connects them rather than listing unrelated scenes.
What to check: Does each scene move the same story forward? Is the route understandable?

Approximate beat: 21–30 seconds: return
Picture intention: The boat reappears at the original window. Repeat the identifying detail and resolve the opening.
What to check: Does the ending make the return clear, rather than merely showing another boat?

WATCH IN THREE SEPARATE PASSES

Accept the animatic when it answers your chosen question, revise the plan when it does not, or stop if the story needs a different approach. A film is a separate job with a separate quote; approving this preview does not authorize it.

1. Watch the sequence without trying to evaluate every detail. Write one sentence describing what happened. If it differs from your intended story, revise the treatment before investing in generated footage.

2. Listen to the narration and compare it with the approved wording. Note unclear names, missing words and awkward pacing with timestamps. Good written narration is not proof of accurate audio delivery.

3. Inspect framing and continuity. Check whether the subject remains visible, the intended visual detail survives, and the camera movement helps rather than distracts. Record defects as observations; do not mark an unrendered future clip as passed.

AFTER THE FIRST PREVIEW

With an otherwise untouched starter account, one 5-credit animatic leaves 2 credits. That is not enough for another 5-credit animatic. If you want another version, check the reported job status, balance and new quote first; do not assume revisions are included.

Save the MP4 within its 7-day download period. Keep the approved treatment and your timestamped observations beside it, but keep your private Kleo key out of shared worksheets. The finished-file deadline is different from the retention of prompts and account records described in Privacy.
