Before your first film.

What does Kleo actually create?

One kind of video: a complete narrated film, in a realistic look (generated live-action footage) or an animation look (a 2D animated film). With your assistant it writes the treatment and the storyboard, generates every shot as moving footage, narrates it and renders an MP4. You get a download link in the assistant, good for 7 days.

How long can a film be?

From 15 seconds to 5 minutes. Choose 16:9 for YouTube or 9:16 for a vertical Short; both render at 4K (3840×2160 or 2160×3840) and 60 fps.

Is the first film free?

Connecting gives you 7 starter credits, with no card. A film costs at least 10 credits (1 credit = 2 seconds), so the starter balance alone does not cover one. Add the €5 pack of 10 credits and a new account has 17: a 30-second Short costs 15.

Does Kleo include narration, music and captions?

One narrator, in English or Italian. No background music, no captions and no on-screen text in the film; add those in your editor if your platform needs them.

Will Kleo publish to my channel automatically?

No. Kleo hands your assistant a link to the finished MP4; you review it, download it and upload it yourself. Kleo never posts to YouTube, TikTok or any other channel.
