The film.

A documentary told as five months of mission logs. On November 14, 2023, Voyager 1 started repeating one pattern of ones and zeros, and every question cost forty-five hours of light-time. One narrator walks through the readout, the dead chip and the code moved by hand in 1970s assembly.

What sits on the frame was decided by this film’s treatment: photographic stills with a slow push-in, hand-drawn amber diagrams, terminal windows, dates on black, two clocks in the corner, 35 mm grain, a 2.39:1 frame, a quiet music bed and subtitles. They are choices made for this documentary, not a standard part of every Kleo film. The standard film has one narrator and nothing else: no background music, no captions and no on-screen text.
