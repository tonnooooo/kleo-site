Connect Kleo to ChatGPT.

These are the tested steps. The connector is created once; from then on Kleo is one switch in the + menu of any chat.

  1. Open Settings → Apps & Connectors (called Connectors in older versions), then Advanced settings, and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Back in Connectors, choose Create: name it Kleo, paste the address below, set authentication to OAuth, and save.
  3. Kleo’s page opens: click Start free and you’re back in ChatGPT. That click is the whole sign-up — the account now exists with 7 credits, with no form and no card.
  4. In a chat, turn on Kleo from the + menu and ask for the video. ChatGPT asks you to confirm before it starts a render.

Ask for the film you want.

Say four things and Kleo can start: the subject, the length, the format and the look. Whatever the request leaves out, Kleo asks for in one message before any planning happens — it never picks a subject or a length for you.

How the production runs.

Kleo is a set of tools that ChatGPT calls in a fixed order. You see every step in the chat, and you keep the last word before anything is spent.

1 · The treatmentkleo_adapt_prompt

Reads your request against the intake and returns the treatment: logline, angle, opening, acts, ending, look, pacing, narrator. ChatGPT tells you the logline and the decisions it took; change whatever you want.

2 · The storyboardkleo_storyboard_guide

Explains how the storyboard is written — direction block, scenes, shots, limits — and ChatGPT writes it under the treatment.

3 · The confirmationkleo_create_video

Checks everything and states the exact credits. ChatGPT asks you to confirm before it starts: this is the only moment credits are spent, and a refused call charges nothing.

4 · The renderkleo_wait_for_video

The film is rendered on a GPU rented for that job. It typically takes 25–35 minutes — the server says so when the job starts — and kleo_wait_for_video or kleo_get_job report the progress in the same chat.

5 · The deliverykleo_get_result

Returns the links. You download an MP4 in 4K (3840×2160 or 2160×3840) at 60 fps; the link is good for 7 days. Kleo never publishes to YouTube, TikTok or any channel — that stays with you.

Follow the render.

Once you have confirmed, stay in the same chat and ask for progress on that job: ChatGPT calls kleo_wait_for_video and reports back. Do not start a second job to check on the first one — every accepted job spends its own credits. When the render is done, ask for the result and download the MP4 within 7 days. If you change your mind while it runs, ask ChatGPT to cancel: kleo_cancel_job stops the render and refunds the unspent credits.

What comes back.

Tether, a 20-second film in Kleo’s realistic look: one unbroken pull-back that opens inside the helmet, on the astronaut’s face and his breath on the glass; a gloved hand comes up, the visor floods red, and the camera keeps backing away as he drifts, arms open, until the gold line running up from his pack meets the station and its solar arrays. If something does not work.

  • No Developer mode in Settings. Custom connectors need ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business or Enterprise, and availability of Developer mode can depend on the account or workspace. If the option is missing, that is the plan or the workspace, not the address.
  • Kleo is not in the + menu. The connector has to be saved in Connectors with authentication set to OAuth, then turned on in each chat from the + menu. Check that the address is exactly https://mcp.kleooai.com/mcp.
  • Authorization failed. Open the Kleo connector again and redo the authorization: Kleo’s page opens, click Start free and you’re back in ChatGPT.
  • ChatGPT asks for a confirmation. That is by design: ChatGPT asks before a render starts, and kleo_create_video has already stated the exact credits. Nothing is charged until you say yes and the job is accepted.
  • Not enough credits. A film costs at least 10 credits and the starter balance is 7. Ask for your account page and add the €5 pack: 17 credits cover a 30-second Short.
  • The render seems slow. 25–35 minutes on a rented GPU is the usual time. Ask for the status of the same job instead of submitting it again; if you want to stop, ask to cancel and the unspent credits come back.
  • The link no longer works. Download links are good for 7 days. Download the MP4 as soon as it arrives and keep your own copy.
  • The request was refused. A film is 15 seconds to 5 minutes, 16:9 or 9:16, in the realistic or animation look, narrated in English or Italian. A refused call charges nothing — adjust the request and ask again.
