Get paid to
create with Kleo.

Make videos with Kleo, post them, and earn credits and cash for your views and for the people you bring.

Apply in the Kleo app Read the agreement

Every creator is a Kleo subscriber first. Pick a plan (Lite is €9 a month), apply, and we review your account by hand. Kleo gives back much more than the plan costs once your posts get real views and the people you bring subscribe. There are no free accounts.

How it works

  1. Subscribe and apply. Pick a Kleo plan, then tell us about your account in the app. We check every answer against the account itself.
  2. Prove the account is yours. Put the code Kleo gives you in your bio until we accept you.
  3. Make and post. Create with Kleo, post on your account with the “Made with Kleo” mark, and send us the link.
  4. Earn. Bonus credits for views, extra credits every month you post, and a share of what the people you bring pay.
  5. Move up. Bring real views and real customers, and your plan becomes free.

Commission

Your level's share of every payment made by people who open their first Kleo account through your link, for 12 months, after refunds and payment fees. Take it as Kleo credits at once, worth 1.5 times the money, or as cash 60 days after each payment, by PayPal or Wise once it reaches €50. A refunded payment earns nothing.

Every post that counts

  • Made with your own Kleo account.
  • The “Made with Kleo” mark in a corner, or Kleo's end card.
  • @kleooai tagged and #kleo in the caption.
  • Your Kleo link in the caption or your bio.
  • Marked as an ad: the paid-partnership label, or #ad at the start.
  • Public for at least 30 days.

What ends your place at once

  • Bought views, bots, engagement groups, fake sign-ups, or sign-ups by your own other accounts.
  • Selling or giving away Kleo credits or accounts, or asking anyone for money in Kleo's name.
  • Somebody's face or voice without their consent, or anything sexual, hateful or illegal.
  • Disputing or charging back a payment to Kleo.

Who can join

  • 18 or older, with a Kleo plan (Lite or higher).
  • A public Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or X account at least 6 months old.
  • 1,000 followers, or a post with 10,000 views.
  • At least 9 posts of your own AI work.
  • A username changed no more than 2 times.
  • One account per person.

The real Kleo creators

Only the accounts listed here work with Kleo. Kleo never sells credits or accounts through a creator, and never asks you for money in a direct message.

Questions

Why do I need a plan?

It keeps the program for real creators. The plan pays for itself once your posts get views and the people you bring subscribe.

Can I get a free account instead?

No. The only free plan is the Pro partner level, and it is paid for by the customers a creator brings.

Who owns my videos?

You do. Kleo may share your posts on its own channels, with credit to you, and asks you first before using one in an ad.

Can I work with other AI tools?

Yes. There's no exclusivity.
