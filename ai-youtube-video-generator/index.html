01One idea

Build a film around one clear idea.

Start with the question your viewer wants answered. Give Kleo the facts, the audience and the point you want the viewer to remember. An explainer needs a clear sequence; a story needs a setting, a change and an ending.

For a landscape channel video, choose 16:9. For a Short, choose 9:16. A film runs from 15 seconds to 5 minutes, with English or Italian narration by one narrator. In a film every shot is generated as moving footage from its own frame, so a 3-minute explainer is a sequence of scenes that move, not a slideshow of stills; an animatic, 15 to 60 seconds and 5 credits, keeps the drawn frames under a moving camera.

03The intake

What Kleo asks before it plans.

Kleo reads your request against a short intake. Whatever the request does not say, the assistant asks in one message and waits for your answers. Nothing is assumed: Kleo never picks a subject, a length, a format or a look for you.

  • SubjectWhat the film is about, and the point the viewer should remember.
  • LengthFrom 15 seconds to 5 minutes.
  • Format16:9 for a YouTube video, 9:16 for a Short.
  • LookRealistic or animation.
  • OptionalAudience, tone, and anything that must appear in the film.

A brief that answers all of it up front, like the ones below, skips the questions and goes straight to the treatment.

04The workflow

A production workflow inside your conversation.

01

Write the brief, answer the intake

Describe your subject, length, format and look, and share the facts and tone you want the film to keep. You are talking to your own assistant; Kleo is the studio behind it. Anything the brief leaves out, the assistant asks for in one message. Audience, tone and what must appear are optional; subject, length, format and look are not.

02

Change the treatment

Before anything is rendered, the assistant shows you the treatment: the logline, the angle, the opening, the acts, the ending, the look, the pacing and the narrator. Change what you want; the film follows it.

03

The storyboard, then the credits

Under that treatment the assistant writes the storyboard: a direction block, the scenes and the shots, within Kleo’s limits. This is where the film’s quality is made. Kleo checks everything and answers with the exact credits before it starts. Nothing is charged until the job is accepted.

04

Render and download

The render runs on a GPU rented for that job, typically 25–35 minutes, and the assistant reports progress, then hands you a link to the finished MP4, good for 7 days. Review it, then upload it to your channel yourself: Kleo never publishes to YouTube or anywhere else.

05Two briefs

A brief you can adapt.

Two briefs that answer the whole intake at once. The first is a 3-minute 16:9 video, 90 credits. The second is a 45-second 9:16 Short, 23 credits. Paste one into your assistant and change the subject.

“Use Kleo to make a 3-minute 16:9 YouTube video explaining how ocean currents move heat around the planet, for curious beginners. Realistic look, English narration, a calm and clear tone. Open with a clear question, explain the mechanism, then finish with one takeaway. Show me the treatment and the credit cost before rendering. Use only the facts I provide or can verify; do not invent statistics.”

“Use Kleo to make a 45-second 9:16 Short about why the sky turns red at sunset, for a general audience. Animation look, English narration, a playful tone. One question at the top, one answer, one image to remember at the end. Show me the treatment and the credit cost before rendering.”

06Duration and credits

Plan the duration and the credits together.

A film is priced by length: one credit covers two seconds, rounded up, with a minimum of 10 credits. A 20-second Short costs 10 credits; 60 seconds costs 30; 3 minutes costs 90; 5 minutes costs 150. An animatic of the same storyboard, 15 to 60 seconds, costs 5 credits flat. The exact number is stated by Kleo before the render starts, and a refused request charges nothing.

Credits come in one-off packs, paid once through Stripe, in euro, with no subscription: €5 for 10 credits, €15 for 35, €40 for 100. Connecting gives you 7 credits with no form and no card: one animatic. A film is made only for accounts that have bought a pack — its clips are generated at Kleo’s expense, so free credits never buy one; with the €5 pack you have 17, the film opens, and a 30-second Short costs 15. If you cancel a render, the unspent credits come back.

A 5-credit animatic is a good way to settle the story and the tone before you spend 90 credits on a longer film.

07The delivery

What you receive.

  • FileOne MP4 in 4K, 3840×2160 for 16:9 or 2160×3840 for 9:16, at 60 fps.
  • LengthThe length you asked for, from 15 seconds to 5 minutes; an animatic 15 to 60 seconds.
  • VoiceOne narrator, English or Italian, male or female.
  • PictureIn a film, every shot generated as moving footage; in an animatic, the drawn frames under a moving camera. No music, no captions, no on-screen text.
  • DeliveryA download link handed over by your assistant, good for 7 days.

Kleo’s film does not include music, captions or on-screen text. If your channel needs them, add them in your editor after the download. Uploading to YouTube remains your step: Kleo has no connection to your channel and never publishes for you. Review the film and your channel’s publishing requirements before you upload.
