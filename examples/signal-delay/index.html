01About this example

The film.

A documentary told as five months of mission logs. On November 14, 2023, Voyager 1 started repeating one pattern of ones and zeros, and every question cost forty-five hours of light-time. One narrator walks through the readout, the dead chip and the code moved by hand in 1970s assembly.

The player contains the opening 0:00–1:02, up to the title. Turn on sound to hear the narration; the subtitles are already part of this excerpt.

What sits on the frame was decided by this film’s treatment: photographic stills with a slow push-in, hand-drawn amber diagrams, terminal windows, dates on black, two clocks in the corner, 35 mm grain, a 2.39:1 frame, a quiet music bed and subtitles. They are choices made for this documentary, not a standard part of every Kleo film. For a new film, confirm the narration, music and subtitle options with your assistant before approving the render.

03Make your own

Make your own.

Connect Kleo to the assistant you already use and ask for a narrated film: the subject, the length, the format and the look, realistic or animation. Kleo asks for whatever is missing, returns the treatment, then states the exact credits before the render starts; nothing is charged until the job is accepted. The finished MP4 arrives as a link that stays good for 7 days.

“Use Kleo to make a 2-minute narrated film about the day Voyager 1 started repeating one pattern of ones and zeros. Realistic look, English narration, 16:9. Show me the treatment and the credit cost before starting the render.”

A 2-minute film is 60 credits, and a film is made only for accounts that have bought a pack; a 60-second animatic of the same storyboard is 5 credits, for every account.
