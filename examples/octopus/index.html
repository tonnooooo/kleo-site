02The brief

The whole request, one line.

Copy it as it is, or keep its shape and change the subject: what it is about, how long it runs, which format, which look, which language.

“Make me a vertical Short, about 40 seconds, animation look, about why octopuses have three hearts and blue blood. English narration.”

In a connected client, kleo_adapt_prompt reads a request like this against the intake (subject, length, format, look; audience, tone and what must appear are optional), asks for whatever is missing, and returns the treatment: logline, angle, opening, acts, ending, look, pacing, narrator. kleo_storyboard_guide then sets out how the storyboard is written, and kleo_create_video checks everything, states the exact credits — 20 for a 40-second Short, 1 credit for every 2 seconds — and starts the render on a GPU rented for that job. Nothing is charged until that call accepts the job.
