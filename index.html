02Kleo Films

A whole film from one message.

01 your words

Direct the story

Tell Kleo the subject, the length and the format, in the app or in your assistant. Kleo reads the brief and asks the rest in one message: the look, film or animatic, the AI upscale, music, subtitles and the narration language.

02 treatment

Read the treatment

Kleo shows you the treatment first: logline, angle, opening, acts and ending. Change what you want before a frame is made.

03 render

Kleo renders

Under that treatment the storyboard is written and Nano Banana Pro draws every frame, each one checked against your request. In a film, Seedance 2.5 turns every frame into moving footage; in an animatic the camera moves over each drawn frame instead. One narrator voices it, in English or Italian. The exact credit cost is stated before anything is charged; nothing is charged until the render is accepted.

04 download

Download and publish

The film renders at 4K 60 fps on a GPU rented for your job. Kleo follows the progress and hands you the link to the finished MP4, good for 7 days. Review it and upload it wherever you publish.

05Behind the scenes

You direct. Kleo renders.

Stay in the chat for the brief, the progress and the link: Kleo works behind the conversation. Nano Banana Pro draws the frames, a vision model checks each one against your request, and in a film Seedance 2.5 turns every frame into moving footage — no slideshow of stills — while one narrator carries it. The render happens at 4K 60 fps on a GPU rented for your job, typically 25–35 minutes. The finished MP4 is the deliverable; you choose when and where to publish it.

  • TreatmentYour brief becomes a logline, an angle, an opening, acts and an ending — shown to you before a frame is made.
  • StoryboardDirection, scenes and shots, written under that treatment; the exact credits are stated before anything is charged.
  • FramesGoogle Nano Banana Pro draws every frame; your own photos can become the character references. A vision model checks each picture against your request before it is used.
  • FootageIn a film, ByteDance Seedance 2.5 generates every shot as moving footage from its own frame, in the realistic or animation look. In an animatic, the same drawn frames with the camera moving over each — no generated clip.
  • SoundOne narrator, in English or Italian, carries the film. A music track and burned-in subtitles when you ask for them. The current Terms specify a small Kleo mark on exported videos.
  • FinishAn MP4 at 4K 60 fps, 16:9 or 9:16, on a link Kleo hands you in the chat — good for 7 days. For a film, an optional AI upscale (Real-ESRGAN + RIFE) sharpens every shot.

“Use Kleo to make a 60-second narrated film about how coffee travels from farm to cup. Realistic look, English narration, 16:9. Show me the treatment and the credit cost before starting the render.”

08FAQ

Before you start.

What is Kleo?

An AI creative studio you use by chatting, at app.kleooai.com. Describe what you want — a video, a picture, a voiceover, music, a sound effect or a complete narrated film — and Kleo proposes a card with the model, the settings and the exact credit price. You tap Generate when it looks right, and everything you make is kept in your library.

Which models can I use?

Video: Seedance 2.5 (the default), Seedance 2.0, Seedance 2.0 Fast, Google Veo 3.1 and Veo 3.1 Fast. Pictures: Nano Banana Pro, Nano Banana 2, Seedream 5 and FLUX.2 Pro, in sets of 1, 2 or 4. Audio: voiceover, music and sound effects. Films: frames by Nano Banana Pro and footage by Seedance 2.5, in 4K 60 fps.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. A new account starts with 5 free credits. Every price is shown on the card before you generate: a 5-second Seedance 2.0 Fast clip at 480p costs 1 credit, for example. Free credits work with Seedance 2.0 Fast and with every picture model except Nano Banana Pro, and every account can make the animatic; the first pack opens every model and the films. Packs are paid once: €5 for 10 credits, €15 for 35, €40 for 100.

Am I charged before I tap Generate?

No. Kleo first shows a card with the model, the settings and the exact credit price. Credits move only when you tap Generate. For a film, you approve the treatment and the cost before the render starts.

Can I still use Kleo inside Claude or ChatGPT?

Yes. Kleo is also a remote MCP server at https://mcp.kleooai.com/mcp: add it to Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Cursor, Claude Code or another compatible client and ask for videos and films in the conversation. How to connect.

Will Kleo publish to my channel automatically?

No. Kleo gives you the finished file; you review it, download it and upload it yourself. Kleo never posts to YouTube, TikTok or any other channel.
