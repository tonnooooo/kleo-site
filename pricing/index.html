02Free plan

Free to start: 7 credits, one animatic.

The free plan is included for everyone: connecting Kleo gives you 7 credits, with no form, no card and nothing to cancel. They buy an animatic, 5 credits: the same treatment, storyboard and drawn frames as a film, the camera moving over each frame, the same narrator and graphic layer, 4K 60 fps, no generated clip. 15 to 60 seconds, 16:9 or 9:16, realistic or animation look; it is also the preview of a film.

A €5 pack adds 10 purchased credits and unlocks film rendering. A 20-second film costs 10 credits; a 30-second film costs 15. After one 5-credit animatic, your 7 starter credits become 2; adding that pack gives a total balance of 12, not enough for a 30-second film. Ask your assistant to confirm the credits eligible for your chosen product and the exact quote before you buy or render.

03Calculate

Calculate the credits.

60 s film = 30 credits

Film: 15 to 300 seconds, one credit for every two seconds, rounded up, with a 10-credit minimum; made for accounts that have bought a pack. Animatic: 15 to 60 seconds, 5 credits flat, for every account. The same rule applies to the realistic and the animation look, in 16:9 or 9:16. Packs are bought separately on your account page.

AI upscale, film only: if you say yes in the intake, Real-ESRGAN + RIFE sharpen every shot on the rendering GPU. It costs the film’s credits again, at least 5 (a 20-second film: 10 + 10 = 20 credits), and the credits come back automatically if it cannot be applied. Without it, the film is delivered in classic 4K 60 fps.

This is the cost of one render, not a pack recommendation. Check your usable balance and film eligibility before checkout. Any further accepted render has its own cost.

04Buying and using

Buying and using credits.

01

Connect Kleo

Connect Kleo and ask the assistant for your account page: it shows your balance and the checkout links.

02

Buy a pack

Buy a pack through the Stripe checkout on that page. Packs are one-off purchases in EUR; there is no subscription. Any pack opens the film; until then the account orders animatics.

03

Ask for the cost

Ask for the cost before you approve a render, film or animatic: kleo_create_video states the exact credits first, and nothing is charged until it accepts the job.

A 5-minute film costs 150 credits: a single 100-credit pack does not cover it. An animatic is 5 credits at any length up to 60 seconds. Your available balance and the account checkout are the final reference before purchase.

A refused call charges nothing. If you cancel a render while it is running, the unspent credits are refunded.

05What is included

What the price includes.

One render is one complete narrated film, or one animatic, rendered on a GPU rented for that job. A film typically takes 25 to 35 minutes, an animatic about 15 to 20; the assistant reports the progress and hands you the link.

  • FilmA narrated film with every shot generated as moving footage by Seedance 2.5 from a Nano Banana Pro frame, in the realistic or the animation look. Priced by length; made for accounts that have bought a pack.
  • AnimaticThe same treatment, storyboard and drawn frames, the camera moving over each frame, the same narrator and graphic layer; no generated clip. 5 credits flat, 15 to 60 seconds, for every account. Also the preview of a film.
  • OutputAn MP4 in 4K (3840×2160 or 2160×3840) at 60 fps, 16:9 for YouTube or 9:16 for a Short. The AI upscale is the one optional extra, film only.
  • PicturesFrames drawn by Nano Banana Pro, each checked by a vision model against your request; your own photos can be the character references.
  • SoundOne narrator, in English or Italian. A music track and burned-in subtitles when you ask for them in the intake.
  • LengthA film 15 seconds to 5 minutes; an animatic 15 to 60 seconds.
  • DeliveryA download link for the finished MP4, good for 7 days: keep a local copy. Kleo never publishes to a channel.
