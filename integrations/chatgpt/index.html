02Connect

Connect Kleo to ChatGPT.

Add Kleo once, then enable it in a new conversation. The steps below follow OpenAI’s documentation checked on 24 September 2026; menus and access can vary by account and workspace.

ChatGPT

Open ChatGPT ↗

Connect Kleo once, then create from your conversation. Manual setup is currently available for accounts with Developer mode.

  1. Open Settings → Security and login and enable Developer mode, if available for your account and workspace.
  2. Open Plugins, choose + to add a plugin, and enter https://mcp.kleooai.com/mcp as the public MCP server URL.
  3. Review the plugin information and complete Kleo’s sign-in when prompted.
  4. Start a new conversation and add Kleo to its tools. Choose the animatic or film prompt below and approve the treatment and cost before rendering.

Instructions checked against OpenAI’s setup guide on 24 September 2026. Access and menus can vary by account and workspace.

Server address https://mcp.kleooai.com/mcp

ChatGPT is OpenAI’s assistant. Kleo is a separate service that ChatGPT reaches through a custom connector; the same address connects from compatible clients including Claude, Claude Code and Cursor — see the connection page.

03The first film

Choose your first creation.

Say four things and Kleo can start: the subject, the length, the format and the look. Whatever the request leaves out, Kleo asks for in one message before any planning happens — it never picks a subject or a length for you.

Choose the product before rendering. Try a free animatic: drawn frames, camera movement and narration, 15–60 seconds, 5 credits. Create a film: generated moving footage, 15 seconds to 5 minutes, with a purchased pack and a quote you approve.

Try a free animatic

“Use Kleo to plan a 30-second animatic about a paper boat returning to its maker. Animation look, English narration, 9:16. Show me the treatment, confirm that the product is an animatic, and check the exact credit cost against my available balance. Wait for my approval before rendering. Do not start a paid film or buy credits.”

Create a film

“Use Kleo to plan a 20-second narrated film about two fictional cars leaving an amber-lit tunnel for a coastal road at golden hour. Realistic look, English narration, 16:9. Show me the treatment, the exact film credit cost, my available balance and whether my account is eligible. Wait for my approval before rendering. Do not buy credits. After rendering, give me the finished MP4 download link.”

  • SubjectWhat the film is about, in plain words. Audience, tone and anything that must appear are optional and welcome.
  • LengthA film 15 seconds to 5 minutes; an animatic 15 to 60 seconds.
  • Format16:9 for YouTube or any landscape screen, 9:16 for a Short or any vertical feed.
  • LookRealistic — generated live-action footage, cinematic — or animation, a 2D animated film. In a film every shot is generated as moving footage from its own frame, in both looks; an animatic of the same storyboard keeps the drawn frames under a moving camera, with no generated clip.
  • NarrationOne narrator, in English or Italian. Before approving the render, ask your assistant which music and subtitle options are available and confirm what will be included. The current Terms specify a small Kleo mark on the video. Review the output conditions before purchasing.
04Production

How the production runs.

Kleo is a set of tools that ChatGPT calls in a fixed order. You see every step in the chat, and you keep the last word before anything is spent.

01 kleo_adapt_prompt

The treatment

Reads your request against the intake and returns the treatment: logline, angle, opening, acts, ending, look, pacing, narrator. ChatGPT tells you the logline and the decisions it took; change whatever you want.

02 kleo_storyboard_guide

The storyboard

Explains how the storyboard is written — direction block, scenes, shots, limits — and ChatGPT writes it under the treatment.

03 kleo_create_video

The confirmation

Checks everything and states the exact credits. ChatGPT asks you to confirm before it starts: this is the only moment credits are spent, and a refused call charges nothing.

04 kleo_wait_for_video · kleo_get_result

The render and the delivery

The film is rendered on a GPU rented for that job. It typically takes 25–35 minutes — the server says so when the job starts — and kleo_wait_for_video or kleo_get_job report the progress in the same chat. Then kleo_get_result returns the links. You download an MP4 in 4K (3840×2160 or 2160×3840) at 60 fps; the link is good for 7 days. Kleo never publishes to YouTube, TikTok or any channel — that stays with you.

05Progress

Follow the render.

Once you have confirmed, stay in the same chat and ask for progress on that job: ChatGPT calls kleo_wait_for_video and reports back. Do not start a second job to check on the first one — every accepted job spends its own credits. When the render is done, ask for the result and download the MP4 within 7 days. If you change your mind while it runs, ask ChatGPT to cancel: kleo_cancel_job stops the render and refunds the unspent credits.

07Credits

Credits and price.

  • On connecting7 credits, no form, no card: one animatic.
  • Per film1 credit = 2 seconds, rounded up, 10 credits minimum, whatever the look: 15 s = 10 · 30 s = 15 · 60 s = 30 · 3 min = 90 · 5 min = 150. A film is made only for accounts that have bought a pack.
  • Per animatic5 credits flat, 15 to 60 seconds, whatever the look, for every account.
  • Packs€5 = 10 credits · €15 = 35 · €40 = 100. One-off Stripe checkout in EUR, no subscription.
  • First ShortA €5 pack adds 10 purchased credits, the cost of a 20-second film. A 30-second film costs 15. If you used 5 starter credits on an animatic, the same pack brings the total to 12, not enough for that 30-second film. Check eligible credits and the quote first.
  • Account pageAsk ChatGPT for your Kleo account: kleo_account gives the balance and the checkout links. The Kleo key it shows is private — whoever has it owns the account, so keep it out of screenshots.

08Troubleshooting

If something does not work.

Still stuck? Write to kleooai@gmail.com.

No Developer mode in Settings.

Developer mode and custom plugin access depend on your account and workspace. Check OpenAI’s current instructions and any workspace restrictions. If the setting is unavailable, ask your administrator or use a compatible client you can access.

Kleo is not in the + menu.

Confirm that Kleo was added through Plugins and its sign-in completed. Start a new conversation and add Kleo to its tools. Check that the server address is exactly https://mcp.kleooai.com/mcp. If the controls differ, follow the current OpenAI setup guide above.

Authorization failed.

Open the Kleo connector again and redo the authorization: Kleo’s page opens, click Start free and you’re back in ChatGPT.

ChatGPT asks for a confirmation.

That is by design: ChatGPT asks before a render starts, and kleo_create_video has already stated the exact credits. Nothing is charged until you say yes and the job is accepted.

Not enough credits.

A €5 pack adds 10 purchased credits and unlocks film rendering. A 20-second film costs 10 credits; a 30-second film costs 15. After one 5-credit animatic, your 7 starter credits become 2; adding that pack gives a total balance of 12, not enough for a 30-second film. Ask your assistant to confirm the credits eligible for your chosen product and the exact quote before you buy or render. A refused request spends no credits.

The render seems slow.

25–35 minutes on a rented GPU is the usual time. Ask for the status of the same job instead of submitting it again; if you want to stop, ask to cancel and the unspent credits come back.

The link no longer works.

Download links are good for 7 days. Download the MP4 as soon as it arrives and keep your own copy.

The request was refused.

A film is 15 seconds to 5 minutes, 16:9 or 9:16, in the realistic or animation look, narrated in English or Italian. A refused call charges nothing — adjust the request and ask again.
