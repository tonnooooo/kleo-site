Tether

One unbroken pull-back. It opens inside the helmet, on the astronaut’s face and his breath on the glass; a gloved hand comes up, the visor floods red, and the camera keeps backing away as he drifts, arms open, until the gold line running up from his pack meets the station and its solar arrays. Signal Delay

A documentary told as five months of mission logs: on November 14, 2023, Voyager 1 started repeating one pattern of ones and zeros, and every question cost forty-five hours of light-time. One narrator walks through the readout, the dead chip and the code moved by hand in 1970s assembly. Photographic stills with a slow push-in, hand-drawn amber diagrams, terminal windows, dates on black, two clocks in the corner, 35 mm grain, 2.39:1, a quiet music bed and subtitles, all of it asked for by this film’s own treatment.

Rendered with Kleo’s realistic look. The full film runs 6 min 55 s; the site shows the opening minute, 0:00–1:02. The full showcase exceeds the current standard five-minute film limit. 4K 60 fps master, shown at 1080p.
