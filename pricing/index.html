Free to start: every new account gets 5 credits, with no card. Then one-off credit packs in EUR, no subscription. Films are priced by length (1 credit = 1.5 seconds, 10 credits minimum, which is a 15-second film) and made for accounts that have bought a pack or redeemed a launch code; the animatic, 5 credits up to 20 seconds and 10 up to 60, is open to every account.

03Free plan

Free to start: 5 credits.

The free plan is included for everyone: a new account gets 5 credits, whether you sign in to the Kleo app with your e-mail or connect Kleo to your assistant, with no card and nothing to cancel. In the app they work with Seedance 2.0 Fast and with every picture model except Nano Banana Pro. They also buy an animatic of up to 20 seconds: the same treatment, storyboard and drawn frames as a film, the camera moving over each frame, the same narrator and graphic layer, 4K 60 fps, no generated clip. 16:9 or 9:16, realistic or animation look; it is also the preview of a film. An animatic draws each picture once, so the free product stays free; the picture check still reports what it sees.

A €5 pack adds 10 purchased credits and unlocks film rendering: exactly one 15-second film. A 20-second film costs 14 credits and a 30-second film 20. Kleo confirms the credits for your chosen product and the exact quote before you buy or render.

04Launch codes

One free 15-second film, before any pack.

For the launch, a code opens the film to an account that has not bought anything yet: one film of up to 15 seconds, realistic or animation, at no charge. Each code works for its first 30 accounts, once per account.

The codes: PRODUCTHUNT, HN, REDDIT, X and TIKTOK. Redeem one in either place:

  • tell your assistant “redeem PRODUCTHUNT” (it calls kleo_redeem), or
  • type it on your account page (ask your assistant for the link).

Then ask for a film of 15 seconds or less; the assistant shows the treatment and the cost first, as always. A code already used up says so and charges nothing. When the code is redeemed, Kleo notes which code it was, to learn which launch channel brought you, and nothing more. New here? Kleo in one page →

05Bring a friend

Share your link, earn credits.

Your referral link

Every account has one: ask your assistant for your Kleo account (kleo_account) or open your account page. A friend who connects through it is linked to you. When they buy their first pack, they get 5 extra credits on it and you get 10. Only a real payment counts, once per friend: gifts and codes never do.

Your finished video

In the Kleo app, what you make appears in the chat and in your library. In an assistant, Kleo gives you the download link the moment the render is done, and your account page lists every video with its links for 7 days. Keep a local copy of what you want to keep: nothing is published anywhere.

06Calculate

Calculate the credits of a film.

60 s film = 40 credits

Film: 15 to 300 seconds, one credit for every 1.5 seconds, rounded up, with a 10-credit minimum; made for accounts that have bought a pack (or, for one film of up to 15 seconds, redeemed a launch code). Animatic: 15 to 60 seconds, 5 credits up to 20 seconds and 10 credits up to 60, for every account. The same rule applies to the realistic and the animation look, in 16:9 or 9:16. Packs are bought separately on your account page.

AI upscale, film only: if you say yes in the intake, Real-ESRGAN + RIFE sharpen every shot on the rendering GPU. It costs the film’s credits again, at least 5 (a 15-second film: 10 + 10 = 20 credits), and the credits come back automatically if it cannot be applied. Without it, the film is delivered in classic 4K 60 fps.

This is the cost of one render, not a pack recommendation. Check your usable balance and film eligibility before checkout. Any further accepted render has its own cost.

07Buying and using

Buying and using credits.

01

Open your account

Open Kleo and sign in, or ask your connected assistant for your account page: both show your balance and the packs.

02

Buy a pack

Buy a pack on that page: pay by card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayPal, through Stripe’s secure checkout. Packs are one-off purchases in EUR; there is no subscription. Any pack opens the film; until then the account orders animatics (and, with a launch code, one 15-second film).

03

See the cost first

In the app, the card shows the exact credits and nothing is charged until you tap Generate. In an assistant, kleo_create_video states the exact credits of a film or animatic first, and nothing is charged until it accepts the job.

Payments are processed securely by Stripe.

A 5-minute film costs 200 credits: a single 100-credit pack does not cover it. An animatic is 5 credits up to 20 seconds and 10 up to 60. Your available balance and the account checkout are the final reference before purchase.

A refused call charges nothing. If you cancel a render while it is running, the unspent credits are refunded.

08What is included

What a film includes.

One render is one complete narrated film, or one animatic, rendered on a GPU rented for that job. A film typically takes 25 to 35 minutes, an animatic about 15 to 20; the assistant reports the progress and hands you the link.

  • FilmA narrated film with every shot generated as moving footage by Seedance 2.5 from a Nano Banana Pro frame, in the realistic or the animation look. Priced by length; made for accounts that have bought a pack, or once, up to 15 seconds, with a launch code.
  • AnimaticThe same treatment, storyboard and drawn frames, the camera moving over each frame, the same narrator and graphic layer; no generated clip, each picture drawn once. 5 credits up to 20 seconds, 10 up to 60, for every account. Also the preview of a film.
  • OutputAn MP4 in 4K (3840×2160 or 2160×3840) at 60 fps, 16:9 for YouTube or 9:16 for a Short. The AI upscale is the one optional extra, film only.
  • PicturesFrames drawn by Nano Banana Pro, each checked by a vision model against your request; your own photos can be the character references.
  • SoundOne narrator, in English or Italian. A music track and burned-in subtitles when you ask for them in the intake.
  • LengthA film 15 seconds to 5 minutes; an animatic 15 to 60 seconds.
  • DeliveryA download link for the finished MP4, good for 7 days: keep a local copy. Kleo never publishes to a channel.
