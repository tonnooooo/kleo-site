the assistant you use.

Kleo is a remote MCP server that turns a brief into a complete narrated film, realistic or animated, from Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Cursor or any other MCP client.

01The server

What Kleo is.

Kleo lives at one address. You connect it once, then ask for a film in the conversation. Your assistant writes the treatment and the storyboard with Kleo’s tools; Kleo checks the request, states the exact credits before anything is charged, renders on a rented GPU and hands back a link to the finished MP4, good for 7 days.

Server address https://mcp.kleooai.com/mcp

Kleo makes a narrated film: 4K at 60 fps, 16:9 for YouTube or 9:16 for a Short, from 15 seconds to 5 minutes, with one narrator in English or Italian, every shot generated as moving footage. Two looks are live on the server: realistic, generated live-action footage, and animation, a 2D animated film. From the same storyboard it also makes the animatic: the drawn frames with the camera moving over each, the same narrator and graphic layer, no generated clip, 15 to 60 seconds — the film’s preview, open to every account.

03Today

What is real today.

  • Two sample filmsTether, 20 seconds in one shot, and the opening minute of Signal Delay, a documentary rendered with Kleo’s realistic look. Both are 4K 60 fps masters shown at 1080p.
  • Two looksRealistic and animation, both live on the server, each with a sample on the looks page; two engine looks, Cinema and Stickman, are shown there too.
  • The priceA film: 1 credit is 2 seconds, rounded up, 10 credits minimum, and it is made only for accounts that have bought a pack, because its clips are generated at Kleo’s expense. An animatic: 5 credits flat, for every account. Packs, one-off and in EUR: €5 = 10 credits, €15 = 35, €40 = 100. Connecting gives 7 credits, with no form and no card: one animatic. With the €5 pack it becomes 17, the film opens, a 30-second Short.
  • Not thereNo app, no dashboard: Kleo is only the server behind your assistant. No background music, no captions and no on-screen text in the standard film. Kleo never publishes to YouTube, TikTok or any channel: you download the MP4 and post it yourself.
