Free AI video generation: what Kleo includes

Kleo is free to connect and currently gives new accounts seven starter credits. Those credits can pay for one five-credit animatic. The generated-footage film is a paid product that requires a credit-pack purchase; the free starting offer should not be confused with unlimited video production.

Try a free animatic with Kleo starter credits

A Kleo animatic uses drawn storyboard frames with camera movement and narration, rather than generated moving clips. The current seven-credit starting balance covers one animatic priced at five credits, making it a specific way to test a story before considering paid production.

Plan an AI video before spending production credits

You can prepare a brief, source pack, treatment and review criteria before authorizing a Kleo render. Planning in an assistant may still be subject to that assistant's own plan or usage limits; no Kleo production charge should be inferred merely from discussing an idea.

Free animatic or paid AI film: choose by deliverable

The meaningful comparison is the output you need. Kleo's starter offer supports an animatic; its paid film adds generated moving footage. Compare those deliverables and the current quote instead of comparing the word free with a price in isolation.
