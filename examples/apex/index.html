Apex.
Two cars, one tunnel.

A 20-second film in Kleo’s realistic look: a yellow supercar and a pale-green classic coupé side by side through an amber-lit tunnel, then out onto a coast road at golden hour. Every frame is generated footage. It plays here muted, by itself, while it is in view — no controls, no full screen.

01The film

Out of the tunnel, into the light.

Playing
Apex20 s · 16:9 · realistic · 4K 60 fps, shown at 1080p

Two cars fill the frame, nose to the camera: a yellow mid-engined supercar and a pale-green classic coupé with round headlamps, helmeted drivers behind the glass, rows of amber lights racing overhead. The tunnel ends and the road opens onto a cliff-side coast at golden hour, the sea flat and bright beside them; the coupé pulls ahead, the supercar hangs in its light, and the shadows of the guard rail sweep across the asphalt.

The copy on this page is 1080p at 60 fps, taken from the 4K master, and it plays muted. The standard Kleo film has one narrator, in English or Italian, and no background music, no captions and no on-screen text.

02What it shows

Six things to look for.

  • Two subjectsTwo cars held side by side through the whole shot: the same two, the same colours, from the tunnel to the coast.
  • LightAmber tunnel light on the bonnets, then the low sun off the sea: the film changes its light without changing its cars.
  • CameraLow and ahead of the cars, backing away at their speed, so the road pours past on both sides.
  • LookRealistic: generated live-action footage, cinematic. The other films in this look are Tether and Signal Delay.
  • Master4K (3840×2160) at 60 fps, 16:9. Shown here at 1080p, 60 fps, muted.
  • Length20 seconds. A Kleo film runs from 15 seconds to 5 minutes; 20 seconds is 10 credits, the minimum a film costs.
