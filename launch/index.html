You approve the treatment and the exact cost before anything renders.

02Connect

Three steps in Claude.

Works in the Claude app and on claude.ai wherever custom connectors are available for your account. Other clients use the same address: every client, step by step.

01 · Add the connector

Open Settings → Connectors, choose Add custom connector, name it Kleo and paste this address:

https://mcp.kleooai.com/mcp

02 · Click Start free

Kleo’s page opens. One button, Start free: no form, no password, no card. You are back in Claude with 5 credits on your new account.

03 · Ask for the film

In a new chat, turn Kleo on from the tools menu, redeem your code and describe the film. Claude shows you the treatment and the cost; nothing renders until you approve.

03Launch code

One free 15-second film.
Before you buy anything.

Each code opens one film of up to 15 seconds for the first 30 accounts that redeem it, once per account: PRODUCTHUNT, HN, REDDIT, X or TIKTOK. Paste this into Claude once Kleo is connected:

“Redeem the Kleo launch code PRODUCTHUNT. Then plan a 15-second realistic film of a paper boat crossing a rain puddle at dusk, 9:16. Show me the treatment and the cost, and wait for my approval.”
