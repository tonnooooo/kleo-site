Kleo makes videos, pictures, voiceovers, music and complete narrated films from a conversation, in the Kleo app at app.kleooai.com. The same Kleo also works inside Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Cursor and Claude Code.

01Kleo

What Kleo is.

You tell Kleo what you want, in your own words, and attach photos, video clips or sound clips when you have them. Kleo answers with a card: the model, the settings and the exact credit price. Nothing is charged until you tap Generate, and what you make is kept in your library.

Video: Seedance 2.5 by default, Seedance 2.0, Seedance 2.0 Fast (the cheapest), Google Veo 3.1 and Veo 3.1 Fast for maximum quality, with photos, clips and sounds as references, 24 camera and effect presets and Prompt Enhance. Pictures: Nano Banana Pro, Nano Banana 2, Seedream 5 and FLUX.2 Pro, in sets of 1, 2 or 4. Audio: voiceover, music and sound effects. Apps: Face Swap, Video Face Swap, Talking Photo, Lipsync, Motion Control, Outfit Swap, Edit Video and Upscale Video.

Kleo Films: a complete narrated film ordered from the chat, 4K at 60 fps, 16:9 for YouTube or 9:16 for a Short, from 15 seconds to 5 minutes, with one narrator in English or Italian, every shot generated as moving footage by ByteDance Seedance 2.5 from a frame drawn by Google Nano Banana Pro. A vision model checks every picture against your request, and your own photos can become the characters. Two looks: realistic, generated live-action footage, and animation, a 2D animated film. From the same storyboard Kleo also makes the animatic: the drawn frames with the camera moving over each, the same narrator, no generated clip, 15 to 60 seconds — open to every account.

In your assistant: the same Kleo is a remote MCP server. Add it to Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Cursor or Claude Code and ask for videos and films in the conversation, with the same account and credits.

Server address https://mcp.kleooai.com/mcp

03Today

What is real today.

  • Four showcased filmsTether, a 20-second single shot; Apex, a 20-second drive from tunnel to coast; the opening minute of Signal Delay; and an animated octopus Short. These are 1080p previews of 4K, 60 fps masters. Each showcase has its own treatment; check its notes against the current offer.
  • Two looksRealistic and animation, both live on the server, each with a sample on the looks page; two engine looks, Cinema and Stickman, are shown there too.
  • The priceA new account receives 5 free credits, with no card required. Every request shows its exact price before you tap Generate: a 5-second Seedance 2.0 Fast clip at 480p costs 4 credits, a 5-second Seedance 2.5 clip at 720p 7, an 8-second Veo 3.1 Fast clip 5 and an 8-second Veo 3.1 clip 12; a 15-second template costs 10 credits at 480p. Free credits work with Seedance 2.0 Fast at 480p and with every picture model except Nano Banana Pro; the first pack opens every model. An animatic costs 5 credits up to 20 seconds and 10 up to 60. A film costs 1 credit for every 1.5 seconds, rounded up, with a 10-credit minimum and a purchased pack required (or, once, a launch code for a 15-second film). Packs are €5 = 10 credits, €15 = 35 and €40 = 100, paid once: the €5 pack is exactly a 15-second film. Check the usable balance and quote first.
  • The modelsIn the app: Seedance 2.5, 2.0 and 2.0 Fast and Google Veo 3.1 and Veo 3.1 Fast for video; Nano Banana Pro, Nano Banana 2, Seedream 5 and FLUX.2 Pro for pictures. In a film: Seedance 2.5 for the moving footage, Nano Banana Pro for the frames and character sheets, Suno for the music track, Kokoro voices for the narration. An optional AI upscale (Real-ESRGAN + RIFE) sharpens a film for its credits again, at least 5.
  • Before you renderKleo never publishes to YouTube, TikTok or another channel: download the MP4 and post it yourself. Kleo asks in one message for the look, film or animatic, the AI upscale, music, subtitles and the narration language; your answers show in the treatment you approve. The current Terms specify a small Kleo mark on the video. Review the output conditions before purchasing.
