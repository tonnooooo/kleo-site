04Behind the scenes

You direct. Kleo renders.

Stay in your assistant for the brief, the progress and the link: Kleo works behind the conversation. In a film every shot is generated as moving footage from its own frame — no slideshow of stills — and one narrator carries it. The render happens at 4K 60 fps on a GPU rented for your job, typically 25–35 minutes. The finished MP4 is the deliverable; you choose when and where to publish it.

  • TreatmentYour brief becomes a logline, an angle, an opening, acts and an ending — shown to you before a frame is made.
  • StoryboardDirection, scenes and shots, written under that treatment; the exact credits are stated before anything is charged.
  • FootageIn a film, every shot generated as moving footage from its own frame, in the realistic or animation look. In an animatic, the same drawn frames with the camera moving over each — no generated clip.
  • NarrationOne narrator, in English or Italian, carries the film. No music, no captions, no on-screen text.
  • FinishAn MP4 at 4K 60 fps, 16:9 or 9:16, on a link your assistant hands you — good for 7 days.

07FAQ

Before your first film.

What does Kleo actually create?

Two products from one storyboard. The film: a complete narrated film, in a realistic look (generated live-action footage) or an animation look (a 2D animated film), every shot generated as moving footage. The animatic: the same treatment, storyboard and drawn frames, the camera moving over each frame, the same narrator and graphic layer, no generated clip — 15 to 60 seconds, also the film’s preview. With your assistant Kleo writes the treatment and the storyboard, narrates and renders an MP4 at 4K 60 fps. You get a download link in the assistant, good for 7 days.

How long can a film be?

From 15 seconds to 5 minutes; an animatic from 15 to 60 seconds. Choose 16:9 for YouTube or 9:16 for a vertical Short; both render at 4K (3840×2160 or 2160×3840) and 60 fps.

Is there a free plan?

Yes: connecting is free and gives every new account 7 credits, with no sign-up and no card — enough for one animatic at 5. Free credits never buy a film: a film costs at least 10 credits (1 credit = 2 seconds) and is made only for accounts that have bought a pack, because its clips are generated at Kleo’s expense. Add the €5 pack of 10 credits and a new account has 17: the film opens, and a 30-second Short costs 15.

Does Kleo include narration, music and captions?

One narrator, in English or Italian. No background music, no captions and no on-screen text in the film; add those in your editor if your platform needs them.

Will Kleo publish to my channel automatically?

No. Kleo hands your assistant a link to the finished MP4; you review it, download it and upload it yourself. Kleo never posts to YouTube, TikTok or any other channel.

Which looks can I choose?

Two. Realistic is generated live-action footage; animation is a 2D animated film. Name the look in your brief and your assistant passes it on — the price depends on the length only, not the look. See the two looks.
